Questions

Before you order.

Does the case change how my pump works?

No. The case is an accessory that holds the device. It does not connect to it, cover its controls, or change how it works. Keep following your care team's and the manufacturer's instructions.

What does "MagSafe-compatible" mean here?

The steel plates in the back of the shell are attracted to magnets, so the case holds to MagSafe-style magnetic mounts, charger stands and car holders. It does not charge anything. Confirm tested accessories

How strong is the strap magnet?

Strong. Keep the strap away from bank cards, hotel key cards and mechanical watches. Keep it away from small children, who could swallow loose magnets.

How do I clean it?

Wipe it with a damp cloth and mild soap. Don't put it in a dishwasher, and don't leave it on a car dashboard in summer. PETG can soften in high heat.
