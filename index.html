From the bench · build log

Every change, including the ones that didn't work.

The iLet case has been through a lot of prints. Here's what changed and why. Draft copy · check details

  1. Rev A

    A see-through shell to check every joint.

    Printed in clear PETG and bonded with JB Weld ClearWeld, so we could see exactly where the glue went and where it didn't.

    Prototype
  2. Plates

    Steel strike plates, cut to spec.

    430 stainless with a #4 finish, laser cut by OSHCut. Magnetic, so the strap and MagSafe-compatible mounts both hold.

    Material
  3. Strap

    E6000 peeled right off the strap.

    Fine on most things, not on foamed TPU. The magnet is now bonded with an MS polymer adhesive instead.

    Failed test
  4. Strap

    A strap that's soft on skin.

    Siraya Tech TPU Air: foamed, matte and light, so it doesn't feel like plastic against you all day.

    Material
