About Phorbie

Made by someone who wears one.

Phorbie started with one part for one pump. Every piece is designed, printed and assembled by hand in California. Founder story placeholder

01

Small batches

Parts are printed and assembled in runs of a few dozen, so colors and fit can change quickly when wearers ask.

02

Plain materials

PETG, TPU, laser-cut steel and neodymium magnets. Nothing hidden, nothing coated.

03

Straight answers

Questions about fit, colors or an order go to a real person. Write to hello@phorbie.com.
