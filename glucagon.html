Questions

Before you order.

Does it come with glucagon?

No. This is the case and liner only. Your glucagon kit comes from your pharmacy.

Will my kit fit?

The liner is shaped for the Gvoke Kit's vial and syringe. It isn't made for nasal glucagon or auto-injector pens. If you're not sure about your kit, write to hello@phorbie.com before you order.

Why is the label on both sides?

So the case is labeled whichever way up it lands in a bag, a drawer or a glove box, and whoever picks it up knows what's inside.

How do I clean it?

Wipe the case with a damp cloth and mild soap. The liner lifts out to rinse. Don't put either in a dishwasher, and don't leave the case on a car dashboard in summer. PETG can soften in high heat.
